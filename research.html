Research

Research

My work concerns algebraic and geometric objects connected to mathematical physics, especially symplectic and Poisson singularities, their resolutions and deformations, Poisson (co)homology, and their connections with representation theory. I have also worked on deformations and quantisations of log symplectic structures.

Current interests

Topics I have worked on include symplectic resolutions and singularities; quiver and character varieties; Poisson geometry and Poisson (co)homology; deformations and quantisations of log symplectic structures; deformation theory; Hochschild and cyclic homology; algebraic differential operators; preprojective algebras; and noncommutative algebraic geometry.
